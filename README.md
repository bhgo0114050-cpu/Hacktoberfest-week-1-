# 🌿 TrailMate

### Touch Grass. Explore More.

TrailMate is a frontend web application designed to encourage people to spend more time outdoors and less time continuously looking at their screens.

The app creates personalized outdoor adventures based on the user's available time, preferred activity, and difficulty level.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌿 Personalized outdoor adventure generator
- ⏱️ Outdoor activity timer
- 🎯 Multiple difficulty levels
- 🚶 Walking challenges
- 🌱 Nature exploration missions
- 📸 Outdoor photography challenges
- 🐦 Birdwatching activities
- 🧘 Mindfulness activities
- 🏃 Outdoor fitness challenges
- ⭐ XP and reward system
- 🔥 Adventure streak tracking
- 📊 Outdoor activity statistics
- 📜 Adventure history
- 📷 Nature discovery photo upload
- 📵 Touch Grass Mode
- 🌙 Dark mode
- 💾 Local storage for saving progress
- 📱 Responsive design for mobile and desktop

---

## 💡 The Idea

Many people spend a large amount of their free time on screens.

TrailMate uses technology for the opposite purpose:

> **The goal isn't to keep you on the app. The goal is to get you off the app.**

The application gives users simple missions that encourage them to walk, observe nature, explore their surroundings, take photographs, and spend time away from their screens.

---

## 🎯 How It Works

1. Open TrailMate.
2. Select how much time you have.
3. Choose an outdoor activity.
4. Select a difficulty.
5. Generate an adventure.
6. Start the outdoor timer.
7. Complete the missions.
8. Earn XP.
9. Track your outdoor progress.

---

## 🌱 Example Adventure

### The Green Explorer

**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Difficulty:** Easy

Missions:

- 🌿 Find two different types of leaves.
- 👀 Find something you have never noticed before.
- 🎧 Stop and identify three different sounds.
- 🌈 Find five different colors around you.
- 📵 Finish your walk without checking your phone.
